# Demo content credits

Bosunarr's demo mode shows only free works: Blender Foundation / Blender Studio open movies (Creative Commons
Attribution, some Attribution-ShareAlike), *Sita Sings the Blues* (Nina Paley, CC BY-SA) and films, cartoons and TV
episodes that are in the public domain in the United States. Titles, years and episode names are factual; every
other piece of data in the demo (libraries, downloads, requests, users, statistics) is made up.

Every image below was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, centre-cropped (or letterboxed) to a poster (2:3, 342 px
wide) or backdrop (16:9, at most 780 px wide) and re-encoded as WebP. Those crops are adaptations: images under a
ShareAlike licence are shared under the same licence. Licence names link to the licence; the source link is the
Commons file page with the full attribution and licence details.
